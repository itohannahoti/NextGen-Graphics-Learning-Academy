AI Graphics Fundamentals

Understand what AI-generated graphics are and how AI works

Start here. Learn the basics of AI image generation, prompts and reference images before you create anything.

What Are AI-Generated Graphics?
+

AI-generated graphics are images created or modified with artificial intelligence. Instead of manually creating everything, you give AI an idea, prompt, or reference image, and it helps produce the visual.

  • Think of AI as your creative assistant — not the designer.
Understanding AI Image Generators
+

AI image tools mainly do three things:

  • Text → Image: describe an idea and AI creates it
  • Image → Image: give AI an image and change it
  • Image + Text → Image: give AI a reference and explain what to change
How AI Understands Prompts
+

A prompt is an instruction you give AI. Instead of "A woman," give more direction.

  • “A confident woman wearing a black suit, standing in a modern office, cinematic lighting, professional atmosphere.”

The more clearly you describe the important parts, the more control you have.

Reference Images
+

Reference images help AI understand what you want — faces, characters, clothing, poses, lighting, colors, composition and style.

  • References are especially useful when you want the same character or visual style across multiple graphics.
Common AI Mistakes
+

AI can produce bad hands, extra fingers, distorted faces, broken text, poor anatomy and inconsistent characters.

  • Don’t start over. Find the problem → tell AI what to fix → regenerate or edit.

Prompting for Graphics

Direct the image like an art director, not just write prompts

Master the skills to control exactly what AI produces.

Think Like an Art Director
+

Before creating an image, ask:

  • What am I creating?
  • Who is it for?
  • What should people notice first?
  • What should it feel like?
  • What should the subject look like?

You are not just writing prompts — you are directing the image.

What to Control in a Prompt
+
  • Subject — who or what is in the image
  • Composition — where everything should be
  • Camera — close-up, wide shot, low angle
  • Lighting — soft, dramatic, cinematic, neon, studio
  • Environment — where is the scene
  • Colors — what palette to use
  • Mood — luxury, mystery, excitement, professionalism
  • Clothing & Pose — what the subject wears and does
  • Style — realistic, cinematic, 3D, illustrated
  • Aspect Ratio — 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9
  • Negative Instructions — what AI should avoid
Basic Prompt Formula
+

Subject + Environment + Composition + Lighting + Style + Mood

  • “A confident woman in a black suit, standing in a futuristic office, centered composition, cinematic lighting, premium realistic style, mysterious mood.”

Don’t make every prompt extremely long. Clear beats complicated.

AI Character Creation

Create consistent characters and turn them into campaigns

Build original characters with AI and keep them consistent across poses, outfits and projects.

Create Your Character
+

Define: face, skin tone, hair, clothing, body type, personality and distinctive features.

Keep the Character Consistent
+

Use reference images and consistent descriptions to maintain facial features, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions and overall identity.

Create Character Variations & Campaign
+

Put the same character into different poses, environments, outfits and expressions. Then turn one character into a full campaign:

  • Character → Poster → Profile Picture → Advertisement → Social Graphics

AI Image Editing

Edit existing images instead of starting from scratch

AI can change what needs changing while keeping what already works.

What AI Can Edit
+
  • Change clothing
  • Change backgrounds
  • Add or remove objects
  • Change poses
  • Fix hands
  • Improve faces
  • Change lighting
  • Extend images
  • Change colors
  • Upscale images

Golden rule: change what needs changing. Keep what already works.

7

Designing Like a Professional

AI creates the image. Design makes it a professional graphic

The design principles that turn a beautiful AI image into a professional graphic.

The Principles of Professional Design
+
  • Visual Hierarchy — make the most important element stand out first
  • Typography — readable fonts, proper sizing, spacing and alignment
  • Contrast — make important elements easy to see
  • Composition — balance elements, avoid clutter
  • Color — colors that work together and support the message
  • Spacing — give elements room to breathe
  • Branding — keep colors, fonts, logos and style consistent
  • Storytelling — a graphic should communicate an idea or emotion, not just look pretty
